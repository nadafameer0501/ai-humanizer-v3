Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Our Privacy Commitment

Free AI Humanizer is designed with privacy as a core principle. We believe your writing data should never be stored, logged, or exploited.

What We Do with Your Text

  • In-memory processing only: Your text is processed on the server and the humanized result is returned to your browser. Nothing is written to disk, stored in a database, or logged.
  • No account required: No user accounts, no profiles, no history. Each session is independent.
  • No third-party sharing: Your text is not shared, sold, or licensed to any third party.
  • No analytics on content: Aggregate usage metrics may be collected, but the actual text you humanize is never sent to analytics.

How the AI Provider Handles Your Text

When you click "Humanize my text," your text is sent to OpenRouter, which routes the request to a free-tier open-source model. OpenRouter's free-tier policies apply. We add $10 of OpenRouter credits once to raise the daily free limit from 50 to 1,000 requests — after that, the free limit resets monthly. For full details, see OpenRouter's terms of service.

Cookies and Tracking

  • Essential cookies only — no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • A "privacy badge" in the UI indicates that nothing is stored.

Your Rights

You can humanize text anonymously without creating an account. If you have concerns about how your data is handled, you can use the tool without providing any personal information.
