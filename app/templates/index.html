What is an AI text humanizer?

An AI text humanizer (also called an AI-to-human text converter or AI paragraph rewriter) takes text generated by an AI such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude and rewrites it so it sounds natural, flowing, and written by a person. It removes the tell-tale AI writing patterns - robotic phrasing, overused words like "leverage", "delve", and "moreover", forced sentence structures, emojis, and filler - while fixing grammar and adjusting sentence rhythm.

Our humanizer is 100% free, requires no signup, and never stores your text. You can humanize up to 2000 words at a time - enough for emails, essays, cover letters, blog posts, LinkedIn posts, assignments, and messages.
